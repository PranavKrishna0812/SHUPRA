Material & Spec

The PLA Medium

We print exclusively in premium PLA (Polylactic Acid) bioplastics, offering custom matte, silk, and organic composite finishes.

Core Polymer

Sustainable PLA Bioplastic

Derived from 100% renewable resources like cornstarch and sugarcane. It is biodegradable, non-toxic, and emits zero harmful fumes, making it the perfect choice for home-safe sacred art and prototyping.

Finish 01

Matte Clay Texture

Diffuses light softly across coordinates, hiding layer lines and highlighting micro-details on sculptures.

Finish 02

Silk Metallic Luster

Polished silk polymer additions create a reflective metallic look resembling cast brass and bronze.

Finish 03

Mineral & Wood Composites

PLA filaments blended with real dust particles (stone gypsum, pine, copper). The finished objects can be sanded, stained, and possess the physical weight and cool-touch of real raw materials.
